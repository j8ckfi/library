# GLM-5: Towards Frontier General Language Models

## Abstract Summary
GLM-5 presents architectural and scaling innovations for general-purpose bilingual foundation models, featuring hybrid attention and robust multi-step pretraining stages.

## Key Contributions
1. **Hybrid Architecture**: Combines local sliding-window attention with global latent attention layers.
2. **Cross-Lingual Pretraining**: Balanced pretraining recipes for high reasoning transfer across languages.
