# Kimi-K2: Trillion-Scale MoE Pretraining with MuonClip

## Abstract Summary
Kimi-K2 presents the pretraining methodology for trillion-token Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, introducing the MuonClip optimizer to prevent gradient spikes and numerical divergence in dynamically routed projections.
