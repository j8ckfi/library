# TrajEvolve

## Abstract Summary
TrajEvolve rewrites low-quality steps into evolutionary trajectories inside the base model's support and labels each step as necessary or redundant by counterfactual verification.

## Key Contributions
1. Hindsight trajectory evolution plus binary necessity credit. NeurIPS 2026.

## Empirical Highlights
- Outperforms sparse-outcome RL on both reported long-horizon datasets.
