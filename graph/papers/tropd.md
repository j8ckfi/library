# TrOPD: Trust-Region On-Policy Distillation for Large Language Models

## Abstract Summary
TrOPD incorporates trust-region bounds into on-policy distillation, preventing catastrophic collapse when student policies explore low-probability teacher branches.

## Key Contributions
1. **Trust-Region Student Updates**: Bounds policy deviation between consecutive distillation iterations.
2. **Robust Multi-Turn Distillation**: Enhanced stability on complex agentic and conversational traces.
