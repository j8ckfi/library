# DCVC-RT: Real-Time Deep Contextual Video Compression

## Abstract Summary
DCVC-RT achieves real-time neural video decoding (>60 FPS at 1080p) while preserving H.266/VVC-parity rate-distortion efficiency. Crucially, long-sequence fine-tuning is required during training to avoid PSNR collapse.
