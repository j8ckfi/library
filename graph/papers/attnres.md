# AttnRes: Attentive Residual Connections for Deep Transformer Scaling

## Abstract Summary
AttnRes replaces static residual addition in deep transformer networks with attentive skip connections, improving gradient propagation through 100+ layer architectures.

## Key Contributions
1. **Attentive Skip Routing**: Parametrized gating across previous layer activations.
2. **Deep Scaling**: Stabilizes training dynamics in ultra-deep transformer stacks.
