# EG-GRPO

## Abstract Summary
Outcome-only search RL under-supervises intermediate evidence gathering. EG-GRPO attributes synthetic evidence claims onto trajectory steps, then mixes discounted step advantage with GRPO and a three-level fallback.

## Key Contributions
1. Evidence-claim step rewards for deep-search agents.

## Empirical Highlights
- BrowseComp / GAIA / xBenchDS evaluation; needs annotated synthetic data.
