# Exploration via Activation-Space Steering for Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

## Abstract Summary
ASE clusters activation differences between correct and incorrect RLVR responses and applies those vectors to later rollouts so a fixed sample budget covers more reasoning paths.

## Key Contributions
1. Online activation-difference steering bank for GRPO-family RLVR.

- Qwen3-1.7B/8B-Base DAPO-Math-17k Mean@8 +4.87 / +1.87 vs GRPO.
