# Elicitation or Expansion? Demystifying Gains from Agent Harness Optimization

## Abstract Summary
Harness optimization gains can be elicitation (more reliable success on already-reachable tasks) or expansion (a broader solvable set). Pass@1 advantages can reverse at larger attempt budgets, and aggregate gains hide hard-task shortfalls.

## Key Contributions
1. Elicitation vs expansion diagnosis for frozen-weight harness optimization.
