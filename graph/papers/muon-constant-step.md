# Constant Step Sizes for Muon-Type Spectral Optimizers on Strongly Convex Quadratics

## Abstract Summary
On strongly convex matrix quadratics, Muon-type updates with a constant step size fall into three regimes from gain and length: bounded-gain (preconditioned GD), unbounded-gain with vanishing length (total-curvature step sizes), and non-vanishing length (almost-everywhere non-convergence). Practical Muon variants occupy all three depending on normalization, not the polar polynomial.

## Key Contributions
1. Gain/length taxonomy for constant-step Muon on quadratics.

## Empirical Highlights
- Does not retarget Muon2. Normalization and scaling choose the regime.
