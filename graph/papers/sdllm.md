# SDLLM: Spiking Diffusion Language Models

## Abstract Summary
SDLLM designs spiking score-based diffusion models for continuous and discrete text synthesis, combining the parallel generation of diffusion with the energy efficiency of neuromorphic spiking.

## Key Contributions
1. **Spiking Denoising**: Discrete spike temporal dynamics mapped to continuous diffusion score matching.
2. **Parallel Sampling**: Non-autoregressive generation with low-energy spiking operations.
