# Latent Geometry: Riemannian Metrics for Continuous Control Latent Spaces

## Abstract Summary
Latent Geometry endows learned model-based RL latent spaces with pull-back Riemannian metrics, enabling geodesic trajectory optimization that respects dynamic physical constraints.

## Key Contributions
1. **Riemannian Latent Planning**: Optimizes control trajectories along geodesic curves in latent space.
2. **Smooth Trajectory Synthesis**: Eliminates sharp action discontinuities in continuous robotic tasks.
