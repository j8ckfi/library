# DCVC-MB: Deep Contextual Video Compression with Multi-Branch Processing

## Abstract Summary
DCVC-MB incorporates multi-branch multi-scale feature decomposition into deep contextual video compression architectures, improving 4K and 8K high-resolution compression efficiency.

## Key Contributions
1. **Multi-Branch Contextual Processing**: Parallel multi-scale branches capturing global motion and fine textural details.
2. **High-Resolution Efficiency**: Significant BD-rate improvements on 4K/8K video sequences.
