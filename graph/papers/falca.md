# FALCA

## Abstract Summary
FALCA trains a Searcher-Falsifier deep-search agent with SFT on falsification traces and agentic RL that assigns hierarchical credit over research stages, state transitions, and actions.

## Key Contributions
1. Propose-falsify architecture plus hierarchical credit for deep search.

## Empirical Highlights
- Gains on five search benches vs backbone/harness ablations; less confirmation bias.
