# CircuitLasso: Sparse Pruning for Causal Circuit Identification

## Abstract Summary
CircuitLasso applies continuous L1 Lasso relaxation to discover minimal causal sub-circuits responsible for specific transformer capabilities, scaling discovery to 70B models.

## Key Contributions
1. **Lasso-Based Circuit Pruning**: Continuous optimization for minimal causal edge discovery.
2. **Faithfulness**: High behavioral faithfulness with extreme circuit sparsity.
