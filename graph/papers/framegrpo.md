# FrameGRPO

## Abstract Summary
Video GRPO that shares frames across a group produces homogeneous rewards. FrameGRPO samples complementary frame sets and applies gated temporal-efficiency credit. Seven video benches vs fixed-frame GRPO.

## Key Contributions
1. Multi-frame rollouts as the video-specific silent-group fix.

## Empirical Highlights
- Fewer all-correct and all-incorrect groups than fixed-ratio GRPO.
