# Qwen3.5-Omni: Unified Multimodal and Speech-Language Architecture

## Abstract Summary
Qwen3.5-Omni details the unified multimodal architecture and Talker RL alignment pipeline supporting real-time streaming speech, vision, and text interaction.

## Key Contributions
1. **Omni-Modal Architecture**: Unified tokenization and attention across audio, video, and text modalities.
2. **Talker Pipeline**: Reinforcement learning recipe specifically designed for streaming speech turn-taking and prosody.
