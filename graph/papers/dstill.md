# DStill

## Abstract Summary
DStill distills a block-diffusion drafter's multi-step refinement into one-step drafting with a partial-to-full mask curriculum, cutting factorization error without extra draft passes.

## Key Contributions
1. Trajectory-consistency distillation for one-step block-diffusion drafts.

## Empirical Highlights
- Up to +28% acceptance length and +25% speedup vs one-step DFlash; up to 6.45x vs AR.
