# A Unified Empirical Study of LoRA Variants and Rank Scaling Laws

## Abstract Summary
A large-scale empirical evaluation of over a dozen LoRA variants across multiple model families and downstream tasks, establishing reliable rank and alpha scaling laws for fine-tuning.

## Key Contributions
1. **Unified Evaluation**: Rigorous iso-compute comparison across LoRA variants.
2. **Rank Scaling Guidance**: Practical scaling laws for adapter rank selection.
