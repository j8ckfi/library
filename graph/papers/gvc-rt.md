# GVC-RT: Generative Video Compression for Real-Time Streaming

## Abstract Summary
GVC-RT applies real-time generative diffusion priors to reconstruct ultra-low-bitrate video streams with photorealistic high-frequency detail.

## Key Contributions
1. **Real-Time Generative Restoration**: Single-step diffusion decoders for real-time video reconstruction.
2. **Perceptual SOTA**: Superior LPIPS and visual sharpness at sub-100 kbps bitrates.
