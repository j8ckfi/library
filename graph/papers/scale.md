# SCALE: Memory-Efficient Full-Parameter Pretraining via Scaled Subspace Projections

## Abstract Summary
SCALE (published at ICML 2026) develops scaled subspace gradient projections for memory-efficient full-parameter pretraining, overcoming the convergence bottlenecks and high rank-switch latencies of GaLore to become the preferred 2026 low-memory pretraining standard.

## Key Contributions
1. **Scaled Subspace Projections**: Smooth gradient projection transitions without periodic SVD stalls.
2. **Full-Parameter SOTA**: Outperforms GaLore in pretraining loss convergence on 24GB GPUs.
