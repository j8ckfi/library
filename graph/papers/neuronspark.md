# NeuronSpark: Event-Driven Spiking Transformer Architecture

## Abstract Summary
NeuronSpark reformulates self-attention mechanisms into sparse spike-driven matrix additions, achieving transformer-grade representation learning with neuromorphic energy characteristics.

## Key Contributions
1. **Spike-Driven Attention**: Replaces softmax floating-point attention with thresholded spike coincidence detection.
2. **Energy Efficiency**: Orders-of-magnitude reduction in theoretical dynamic energy on neuromorphic chips.
