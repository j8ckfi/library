# Neural Video Compression with Diverse Contexts

## Abstract Summary
Published in IEEE TPAMI, this work introduces DCVC-DC (Deep Contextual Video Compression with Dual Context). By combining temporal context mining with a dual-context spatial-channel entropy model, DCVC-DC is the first neural video compression framework to outperform the traditional H.266/VVC standard reference encoder (VTM) in BD-rate across both PSNR and perceptual quality metrics.

## Key Contributions
1. **Diverse Context Exploration**: Formulated joint spatial-channel and temporal context estimation for latent distribution modeling.
2. **Contextual Residual Paradigm**: Replaced traditional pixel-domain residual subtraction with feature-domain contextual conditioning.
3. **SOTA Benchmark Overhaul**: Outperformed standard VVC reference benchmarks.
