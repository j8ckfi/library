# LSPD

## Method Overview
Reverse-KL OPD is KL-regularized RL with teacher-induced reward \(R=\log(\pi^E/\pi^{\mathrm{ref}})\). Policy-gradient OPD implementations stay on-policy and throw away old student traces. LSPD matches student and teacher token log-probabilities with a Huber quadratic and adds entropy, which is well-defined on prefixes generated by earlier student snapshots:

\[
\ell_t=\rho_\delta\bigl(\log\pi_\theta(y_t\mid h_t)-\log\pi^E(y_t\mid h_t)\bigr)-\eta\,\mathcal{H}(\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid h_t)).
\]

Multi-update on the current batch is the cheap operating point. LSPD-RB keeps a replay buffer of student trajectories and reaches OPD-level Avg@16 with about a quarter of the rollouts. Idealized optimistic LSPD has \(\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\log K)\) regret. This is not CISPO and not Open-MOPD.

## When to Use
- Teacher OPD when rollout cost dominates and you can reuse student traces (multi-update or replay) without dropping entropy.

## When NOT to Use
- Default single-teacher matching → `method:opd`. Multi-teacher budget → `method:open-mopd`. Pass@1 RLVR → `method:cispo`. Latent collapse schedule → `method:lastopd`.

## Relation to Existing SOTA
- Active plug-in on `task:student-distillation` beside `method:opd`. Does **not** enter `current_sota`. Does **not** replace OPD, Open-MOPD, CISPO, or LastOPD.

## Gotchas & Failure Modes
- Off-policy reuse needs the Huber + entropy pair; dropping entropy collapses Pass@k in the paper's diversity plots.
- LSPD-RB is not a free lunch on a stale buffer from a much older student; the paper's 25% figure is first-quarter batches of the same run.
- Quadratic matching is IPO-adjacent in form but matches student–teacher logp, not preference log-odds. Do not swap in SimPO/IPO code.
