# OLMo 3 and Dolma 3: Next-Generation Open Foundation Models and Data

## Abstract Summary
OLMo 3 presents a complete open-source foundation model framework including the Dolma 3 open pretraining data mix, advanced curriculum annealing recipes, and the Dolci post-training instruct pipeline, outperforming OLMo 2 and establishing the leading open recipe.

## Key Contributions
1. **Dolma 3 Open Data Recipe**: Curated multi-trillion token pretraining corpora with open artifacts.
2. **Dolci Instruct Pipeline**: High-quality open supervised fine-tuning and verifiable reward alignment.
3. **Curriculum Design**: Enhanced two-phase pretraining curriculum with high-signal annealing.
