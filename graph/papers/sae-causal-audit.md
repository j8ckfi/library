# SAE-Causal-Audit: Rigorous Auditing of Sparse Autoencoder Causal Claims

## Abstract Summary
SAE-Causal-Audit presents a standardized benchmark suite and auditing protocol to test whether sparse autoencoder dictionary features exhibit true causal necessity and sufficiency during model inference.

## Key Contributions
1. **Standardized Causal Auditing**: Rigorous intervention protocol measuring causal faithfulness vs observational correlation.
2. **Feature Evaluation**: Automated scoring of SAE dictionary quality and monosemanticity.
