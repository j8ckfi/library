# Nemotron-Cascade: Staged Alignment and Distillation for Reasoning

## Abstract Summary
Nemotron-Cascade presents a multi-stage post-training methodology sequencing supervised fine-tuning, reasoning distillation, and preference optimization.

## Key Contributions
1. **Cascaded Stage Transitions**: Smooth progression from broad conversational SFT to complex mathematical reasoning.
2. **Distillation Integration**: Blends teacher logits into intermediate preference optimization rounds.
