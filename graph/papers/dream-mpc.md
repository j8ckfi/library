# Dream-MPC: Accelerated Model Predictive Control with Latent Trajectory Optimization

## Abstract Summary
Dream-MPC (accepted at ICML 2026) replaces expensive sampling-based trajectory optimization (such as MPPI) in latent world models with accelerated trajectory planning, dramatically cutting online control latency.
