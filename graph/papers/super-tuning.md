# Super-Tuning: Subspace-Preserving Parameter-Efficient Adaptation

## Abstract Summary
Super-Tuning constrains parameter-efficient updates to stay within the principal subspace of the pre-trained model representations, minimizing out-of-distribution performance degradation.

## Key Contributions
1. **Subspace Preservation**: Constrained low-rank projections aligned with pre-trained spectral geometry.
2. **OOD Robustness**: Superior out-of-domain generalization compared to unconstrained LoRA.
