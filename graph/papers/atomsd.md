# AtomSD

## Abstract Summary
AtomSD extracts Intent/When/Do/Avoid procedural atoms from successful agent traces and distills them with atom-conditioned supervision plus task-reward optimization.

## Key Contributions
1. Fine-grained reusable procedural atoms for agent self-distillation.

## Empirical Highlights
- Up to +9.31 average points over the strongest reward-based / distillation baseline.
