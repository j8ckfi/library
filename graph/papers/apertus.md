# Apertus: Transparent Open Foundation Architecture with Fine-Grained Routing

## Abstract Summary
Apertus introduces a fully transparent open foundation model architecture featuring fine-grained expert routing and reproducible pretraining artifacts.

## Key Contributions
1. **Fine-Grained Routing**: Sparse modular activations with low routing interference.
2. **Reproducible Open Architecture**: Complete specification of layer configurations and training hyperparameters.
