# CPPO: Constrained Proximal Policy Optimization for Safe and Fact-Grounded Reasoning

## Abstract Summary
CPPO formulates language model reinforcement learning under explicit safety and factual consistency constraints using adaptive Lagrangian multipliers.

## Key Contributions
1. **Lagrangian Constrained RL**: Enforces hard constraints on hallucination and safety violations during reasoning.
2. **Dual Gradient Updates**: Simultaneously optimizes reward while guaranteeing constraint satisfaction.
