# SASA: Subspace Sparse Autoencoders for Neural Circuit Decomposition

## Abstract Summary
SASA introduces Subspace Sparse Autoencoders, overcoming the limitation of 1D vector dictionaries by extracting multi-dimensional subspace dictionaries that model non-orthogonal circular, rotational, and continuous feature representations in transformers.
