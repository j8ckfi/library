# VeriGate: Gated Process Supervision for Mathematical Reasoning

## Abstract Summary
VeriGate demonstrates that un-gated Process Reward Models (PRMs) introduce reward hacking, and shows that PRMs must be strictly gated behind trusted deterministic outcome verifiers to safeguard reasoning alignment.
