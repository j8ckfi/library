# Randomized Momentum Transport with Cheap Predictors

## Abstract Summary
Low-priority theory note on `task:llm-pretraining-optimization`. O(T^{-1/3}) rates for Muon / sign-style LMO updates with randomized momentum transport. Experiments are small-scale (MNIST1D / CIFAR-10). Does not retarget Muon2.

## Key Contributions
1. Randomized momentum transport analysis for Muon/sign LMO.
2. O(T^{-1/3}) rate under the paper's assumptions.
3. Small-scale MNIST1D / CIFAR-10 checks only.
