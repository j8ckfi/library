# Towards Monosemanticity: Decomposing Language Models with Dictionary Learning

## Abstract Summary
Anthropic researchers demonstrate that overcomplete Sparse Autoencoders applied to the residual streams of transformer language models can extract interpretable, monosemantic feature vectors from dense, polysemantic neural representations.

## Key Contributions
1. Established the feasibility of large-scale sparse dictionary learning on transformer representations.
2. Verified monosemanticity and causal steerability of learned feature directions.
