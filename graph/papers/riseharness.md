# Harness Self-Evolution for Long-Horizon Terminal Agents

## Abstract Summary
RiseHarness iteratively edits a frozen-weight terminal harness by analyzing mixed success/fail runs with runtime-monitor traces and importing mechanisms from other harnesses after behavioral checks.

## Key Contributions
1. Mixed-outcome harness RSI with a handbook linking traces to source.

## Empirical Highlights
- Evaluated across models with transfer to unseen benches; ablations of execution evidence and external research.
