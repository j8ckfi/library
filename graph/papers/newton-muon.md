# Newton-Muon: Second-Order Curvature Corrections for Orthogonal Momentum

## Abstract Summary
Newton-Muon introduces low-rank Hessian diagonal curvature approximations into the Newton-Schulz orthogonalization pipeline, accelerating optimization on poorly conditioned weight projections.

## Key Contributions
1. **Curvature Correction**: Merges second-order curvature approximations with matrix normalization.
2. **Step Efficiency**: Improves loss convergence on ill-conditioned attention projections.
